Open MatchDrop
If it doesn't open when you tap the button:
1. Make sure this phone is on the same Wi-Fi as the Mac (or the Mac is on this phone's hotspot).
2. Make sure MatchDrop is open on the Mac.
3. If your phone asks to find devices on your local network, tap Allow.
Then come back here and tap the button again.
